# Notes for working on this repo

- Product owner: the PHO & CO owner. Confirm any new feature idea with them before building it.
- Sites: EAS (Eastcote, restaurant), WEM (Wembley, production, Reg 853 approved), SYD (Sydenham, closed until it reopens and left out of the pilot).
- Keep business rules in `src/lib/domain/`, pure and tested. UI and actions call them; they never re-implement them.
- Every data change goes through `Store` (`src/lib/data/store.ts`). Add the method to the interface, `demo-store.ts` and `sql-store.ts`.
- Server actions in `src/app/actions.ts` must check the session (`requireCtx` / `requireOwner`), validate input with zod and write an audit entry for anything a manager or an inspector would care about.
- Food-safety records are append-only. Correct by adding a new entry, never by editing.
- No staff personal data, PINs or secrets in the repo. Demo people are labelled "(demo)".
- Before pushing, run `npm run typecheck && npm run lint && npm test && npm run build`.
- Brand: cream `#F6F1E7`, green `#2F4A3A`, the logo's teal `#639C8D` with a pale tint `#D6E7E1` for surfaces (tokens `--teal`, `--teal-tint`, `--teal-ink`; no sage), amber `#FCBA63` as the highlight, orange `#C2611A` for urgent. Fonts Barlow Condensed (headings), Be Vietnam Pro (body).
- Database: Supabase project `phoco-ops-hub` (ref iklgabptdlwjwwngepyc, eu-west-2 London). The org's other project ("Pho & Co's Project") is a separate purchase-ledger app: never touch it.
- Schema changes go in a new numbered file in `supabase/migrations/` and are applied to Supabase too.
- Rota: the app never writes to the rota sheets. Imports arrive at `/api/rota/import` (per-site token, hashed in `rota_sources`). Rules in `src/lib/domain/rota.ts`; times are London wall-clock (`src/lib/domain/time.ts`). The sheet sends hourly; the Owner screen warns when one has sent nothing for over `ROTA_STALE_HOURS` (`rotaGoneQuiet`).
- Secrets the server generates (VAPID push keys, scheduler token) live in `app_secrets`; the Supabase cron job `ops-hub-reminders` reads the `cron` token from there.
- Kitchen handover list (`prep_items`; rules in `src/lib/domain/prep.ts`): two lists, To do and To prep (`kind`), in groups (`grp`), edited in the app at `/manage/handover-list` (one list for every site). Managers add and edit (`canEditPrepList`); the owner and ops take items off and put them back (`canRetirePrepItem`, `active`). Checked by `cleanPrepItem`; order: to do first, groups by their first item, then position (`prepGroups`, `filterPrep`, `moveTarget`). Items are never deleted or renumbered (database guard). `PREP_MASTER` (migration 0006) and `BAR_ITEMS` (0024, from the retired Bar & Counter Prep checklist) are the seeds; tests keep them in step. Submitted handovers and their entries are kept (database guards).
- Handover tab (`src/app/handover/`) = notes (`handover_items`) + prep handover. Note rules (which notes show, which go on a report) are in `src/lib/domain/handover.ts`. Old `/prep` links redirect there. Who an item is for (`assigned_to/by/at` on `prep_entries` and `handover_items`; rules in `src/lib/domain/assign.ts`, loaders in `src/lib/handover-service.ts`): anyone with a full session at the site (the tablet) gives a sent prep item or an open needs-action note to someone active there, or changes it (`assignHandover`, audited); draft picks go through `prepEditDraft` (`assignee`); a carried item keeps who it's for (`planSubmit`), and the review step changes it on the waiting item, never by picking it again; someone no longer givable counts as nobody (`stillFor`). The pick list (`pickList`) puts who works the shift first (`targetPeriod`/`pickPeriod`; mornings to 15:00, evenings to 05:00; at least an hour of it). Managers read `/handover` on their own phone (`handoverView`, `phoneHandoverSites`, read-only; `phoneShows`); My shifts and shift reminders show what's for you (`forYou`, `forYouLine` in `reminders.ts`, phone rules kept).
- Chrome (`src/components/Chrome.tsx`): `TopBar`/`PhoneTopBar` take the screen's `title` (then the page renders no h1 of its own), an `eyebrow` (site · date by default), a `back` link and children beside the clock (`Clock`, London time, client-only); `Avatar` (initials, `tone` from `toneOf`: FOH amber, BOH/PROD teal, leads green), `Logo` (PNGs under `public/brand/`). `BottomNav` is a row of condensed labels from 700px. Pages opt into a wide layout with `main.wide`; the default `main` stays 760px until a screen is re-laid out.
- Sign in (`/login`; rules in `src/lib/domain/login.ts`): on a registered tablet, `loginGroups` lists today's rota first (earliest start, with lane and times) then everyone else at the site by name, frozen last; tap a name, then the PIN pad. An unregistered device gets `SetupScreen` (set-up code first on a tablet, staff-ID sign-in first on a phone; `?setup=1` shows it in demo mode).
- Shift Mode (`/shift`, the home for everyone but owners and ops; rules in `src/lib/domain/shift-mode.ts`, loader `src/lib/shift-service.ts`, screen `src/app/shift/`). After sign-in the person's shift on today's published rota (`shiftNow`: running, just ended within `SHIFT_GRACE_MIN`, else next today; a tablet only its own site) decides the lane (`laneOf`: BOH = Kitchen, FOH = Front of house, PROD = Production) and the window; `shiftItems` lists only that shift's things in time order (checks of the lane due within `WINDOW_MARGIN_MIN` of it, or overdue; prep: whole list for BOH/PROD, own items for FOH; notes for them or for nobody; allergen changes, SOPs to read, training; ready to open on morning shifts; the gas check for closers; kit down; tasks), states late → now (`SOON_MIN`) → later → whenever → done. Not on the rota: `laneChoices` (own sections; a manager with none gets the site's) and the period from `periodAt`. Managers get every lane with people on (`lanesToday`, `siteLanes`) side by side with a View as switch; the owner screen's first glance is `RightNow`. Phone rules (`phoneNote`) apply off the tablet. Demo mode seeds this week's rota (`demoRota`, not under vitest). Nothing is written from the screen: its buttons are the app's existing actions.
- Team rota (`/rota`): `buildTeamWeek` in `src/lib/domain/rota.ts`. Staff see published weeks with times only; paid hours (`shifts.paid_hours`, from the Export tab) and draft weeks are for owners and managers. Works for both session scopes (tablet and own phone).
- Roles (`person_sites.role`, per site): owner, ops (operations manager), manager, staff. `isOwner`/`isOps`/`isLead` and `leadSites` are in `src/lib/domain/access.ts`; `managedSites` includes ops. An operations manager gets the overview (`/owner`, `requireLead()` in `src/lib/session.ts`, their sites only), staff and manager accounts at their sites (`canManageAccount`, `canGiveAccess`, `givableRoles` in `staff.ts`), tablets at their sites, and the full app on their own device. Owner-only: closing/reopening sites (and lifting a closure's freeze: `canUnfreeze`, `canMarkLeft`, `canRejoin`), rota sheet connections, right to work (and accounts that can see it), owner and ops accounts. A full session off a tablet needs `runsSite` (owners anywhere, ops at their sites); `remoteSignIn` picks where a staff ID sign-in lands and `canSwitchTo` limits "Work at". Their time off is decided by an owner (`canDecide` takes the requester; `timeOffDeciders`, `seesRequest`). Their activity log: `activityFor` in `activity.ts`.
- SOP library (`/library`; `library_docs`, `library_versions`, `library_acks`, `media`; rules in `src/lib/domain/library.ts`; loaders in `src/lib/library-service.ts`). Versions go draft → in_review → published → superseded (or discarded); one published and one open version per document (unique indexes); database triggers allow only those moves, refuse changing a published version except to mark it superseded, and nothing is deleted (a document's code never changes). Every change names the revision it was shown (`rev`) and is refused if it has moved on (the page offers the latest: `stale`). Writers: `canDraft` (managers and leads); approvers: `canApprove` (owner, ops). An approver acts for every site an SOP covers (`docReach`: its published version's sites and the new version's, so narrowing it still counts; `coversReach`): approving or sending back (`canReview`, never a version you wrote, changed or sent: `contributors`; owners may), taking back or discarding someone else's version (`canWithdraw`, `canDiscard`), retiring and bringing back (`canRetire`, `canReinstate`). The store re-checks contributors when publishing; publishing, discarding and retiring lock the document row before its versions. Discarding the only draft of a never-published SOP retires it. Who reads a version: `readsAt` (staff by sections, managers by the site's sections, never owners, open sites only); acks are per version, and only the approver is counted as having read it. Lists and counts load `libraryDocs(kind, { light: true })` (sections, no steps). The editor keeps unsaved changes in sessionStorage (per tab, per person) and offers them back; `createDraft`/`saveDraft` return an error rather than redirecting when the session has ended (the editor shell is `DraftForm`, shared by every kind). Photos (JPEG only): `POST /api/media` (writers, full scope; ≤ 2 MB read with a cap, real size from `jpegSize`, `PHOTO_DAILY_LIMIT` a day each, checked again atomically in `addMedia`), `GET /api/media/<id>` (anyone signed in; cached 10 minutes, then an ETag check). The owner can erase one (`erasePhoto`, audited; it shows as removed everywhere and is dropped from a draft when it's saved, `dropPhotos`); photos no version uses (discarded drafts don't count) are cleared after `PHOTO_KEEP_UNUSED_DAYS` by `tick()` (09:00–10:00), and the database refuses deleting one in use. The `body` JSON is checked by `cleanSop` (always) and `readyForReview` (to send or publish).
- Recipe handbook (`/library/recipes`, `/library/allergens`; rules in `src/lib/domain/recipes.ts`): prep recipes (`prep`: a batch with what it makes, ingredients, method, storage, shelf life) and dish specs (`dish`: one portion's components, build steps, kitchen notes, dietary tags) are library kinds with the same drafts, versions and four-eyes rules as SOPs (`isKind`, `codePrefix`: PREP-…/DISH-…), but only the owner approves, sends back, retires, brings back, or takes back/discards someone else's version (`approvesKind`, `ownerApproves`; every approval check takes the kind). Shown as one screen-sized card (`RecipeCard`, styles "Recipe card" in globals.css; landscape and portrait tablets, phones scroll, Print A4 with long recipes (`long`) in plain flow): ingredient pictures (`Item.photo`, reused by `ingredientKey`/`ingredientPhotos` via `editorLists`), steps in a grid (`cardPanels`, `CARD_STEPS`), notes as ticks (`noteLines`), a dish's labels on its photo (`callouts`, percent x/y) and serving checks (`standards`); a version not in use is stamped and can't be printed. `photoIds`/`dropPhotos` include ingredient pictures. Bodies are cleaned by `cleanRecipe` and checked by `recipeReady` (every one of the 14 allergens plus E120 marked: contains / can be left out (dishes) / free; a dish needs only its menu section and allergens, so it can reach the matrix before its portion and build are written). Amounts are a number and a unit (`Item.qty` optional: "a drizzle"), scaled with `scaleQty`/`formatQty`/`factorFor`/`inUnit`. Recipes are only on the full app, for writers and kitchen staff (`seesRecipes`: BOH or PROD at an open site, any kitchen's recipes); front of house and own phones get a dish's allergens (`/library/allergens/<id>`) instead. A dish component may link to a prep recipe (`Item.prep`, checked on save); `dishWarnings` flags tags or linked recipes that disagree (it doesn't block). The matrix (`allergenMatrix`, `fitFor` for "a guest avoids") is for everyone. Publishing a dish whose allergens changed (`allergenAlert`) dates it (`library_versions.allergens_changed_at`, kept by later versions); everyone at the dish's sites but owners (`alertedAt`) confirms once (`allergenAlerts`: an ack on any version of that dish after the change counts; acks carry `docId`), shown on Today and My shifts; managers see who has (`alertStats`).
- Training Academy (`/library/training`, `/manage/training`; `training_skills`, `training_questions`, `training_pathways`, `training_starts`, `training_events`; rules in `src/lib/domain/training.ts`; loaders in `src/lib/training-service.ts`). Skills sit in pathways (FOH/BOH/PROD, in `position` order, under a heading `grp`) and may link library docs (staff see only published ones), a video (https link only) and a quiz. A person's passport (`passport`, `progress`) is worked out from append-only events (quiz, done, confirmed, reset): a knowledge skill completes on a passed quiz (or "done" while it has no quiz; `canMarkDone` refuses while drafted questions are waiting), a hands-on one on a manager's `confirmed` after it's ready (`readyAt`: a passed quiz, or "done" only while it has no approved quiz). A refresher is due on its date; from `REFRESHER_SOON_DAYS` before, it can be renewed (`renewal`; for hands-on, readiness only counts from then). A linked doc's major change (`libraryMajorChanges`; only changes after it was linked count: `link_since`) or a manager's take-back (`reset`, with a note the person sees; for a skill only ready, it clears readiness) leaves `REDO_DAYS`. Tightening a skill later is dated so it's fair without clearing what's overdue: `added_at` (added, moved pathway, brought back) is the earliest a deadline runs from, `induction_from` and shorter pathway deadlines (`training_pathways.changed_at`, `induction_was`, `all_was`) give the new days from the change (no later than before), `refresher_from`/`refresher_was` give `REDO_DAYS` (no later than under the old refresher), and a first quiz after someone said they were ready gives `REDO_DAYS` from `quizSince` (`why: "quiz"`). Only the latest change of each kind is remembered. Ready isn't overdue. Deadlines run from the latest `training_starts` row (a new one, never changed, whenever someone starts training in a section again: `withStarts` in both stores around createPerson, setAccess, updatePerson and setSiteStatus; a freeze on its own doesn't count; anyone training with no start gets today's from `startMissingTraining`, every `tick()`, which is how everyone starts on go-live; until then a passport counts from today) or `added_at`, whichever is later; coming back also leaves `REDO_DAYS` for anything to do again. Trainees (`trainingSections`): active staff and managers at open sites, never anyone who is an owner or ops anywhere. Who confirms or takes back: `managesTraining` (owner; ops at the trainee's site; a manager there with that section; never themselves) and `canConfirmNow`. Editors (`canEditTraining`): owner and ops; a question they write is approved at once, drafted ones (`approved_by` null, from an import) are only asked once approved. Quizzes are shuffled and marked on the server (`gradeQuiz`: the pass mark exactly, no rounding up); the right answers never go to the browser; an attempt names the `quizVersion` it was shown and is refused if the quiz has changed. Skills and questions are retired, never deleted (database guards); a retired skill can't be saved. Reminders (`trainingReminders`, kind `training`, keyed by `whyOn`) are queued by `tick()` between 09:00 and 10:00.
- Manager tools live under `/manage` (`requireManager()` in `src/lib/session.ts`; `managedSites`/`canManage` in `src/lib/domain/access.ts`). Tiles and their counts: `src/app/manage/ManageTiles.tsx` and `counts.ts`. Owners see the same tiles on `/owner`.
- Inspection records (`/manage/records`, rules in `records.ts`) and the weekly report (`/manage/report`, `report.ts`, loaded by `src/lib/report-service.ts`, also used for the Monday message) only read existing records. A site's checklists count from its first recorded run (`trackingFrom`); only checklists with a due time are expected daily.
- Team notices (`announcements`, `announcement_reads`; rules in `notices.ts`), time off (`time_off`; `timeoff.ts`) and certificates (`staff_certificates`; `certificates.ts`). Certificates and notices are never deleted: they're ended or marked removed. Right to work is private: only people with `people.sees_right_to_work` (today, just the owner) can see or record it (`canSeeRightToWork`), and it has no document number.
- Messages to people go through `queueNotice` with a `dedupeKey` (unique across the table, so include the recipient). Kinds are listed in the `notifications_kind_check` constraint (migration 0008); add new kinds there too. Certificate reminders are worked out in `tick()` between 09:00 and 10:00 London. Quiet hours are 22:00–08:00; urgent messages (kit not working, P1 tasks) go out until midnight (`afterQuietHours(now, true)`).
- Equipment (`equipment`, `equipment_events`; rules in `equipment.ts`): faults, updates, fixes and services are separate append-only events (a database trigger refuses changes; a fault is fixed once). Anyone at the site can report (either session scope); updates and fixes need the full scope; the register and services are for managers. QR labels open `/equipment/<id>`; `safeNextPath` is the only way back after sign-in.
- Tasks (`tasks`; rules and the task centre's priorities in `tasks.ts`; gathered per site by `src/lib/task-centre.ts`). The centre only reads other modules' records and links to them; only manual tasks and alert resolution are acted on there. Tasks are done or cancelled, never deleted.
- Shift reminders and replies (`shift_replies`; rules in `reminders.ts`; `queueShiftReminders` in `rota-service.ts`, run by `tick()` every time; today's team in `src/lib/team-today.ts`). One reminder per person per shift (`reminderKey`, includes the start time), 2 hours before, ignoring quiet hours; the owner's "Send reminders now" sends the rest of today's early. What goes on a phone comes from `waitingFrom`: never staff notes, complaints only as a count. Replies are append-only (latest counts); late and can't-make-it go to managers (`replyMessages`, `replySendAfter`).
- Daily loop (rules in `daily.ts`; inputs gathered by `src/lib/daily-service.ts`; scheduler jobs `queueGasChecks`, `queueReadyAlerts`, `queueDailyDigest` in `rota-service.ts`). A site is only checked on days with published shifts; ready to open also waits until the site has recorded its first checklist (`checklistsStartedOn`, like `trackingFrom`). `sites.ready_by` is the ready-to-open time. `gas_checks` is append-only, one per site per business date (the day the last shift started; the first tap counts). Gas reminders and alerts ignore quiet hours.
- Shift cover (`cover_requests`, `cover_offers`; rules in `cover.ts`; read helpers in `src/lib/cover-service.ts`; page `/manage/cover`). Same site and section, not working or already approved to cover then (any site, the night before and after too), not on approved time off. Where a request stands (`coverState`) is worked out from the row and the shift as it is now, and lists only load shifts that haven't long ended (`shiftEndsAfter`). One request in play per shift (unique index); one live offer per person; offers only while open. Approved becomes "done" once the import shows the coverer on; managers can cancel an approval. The reason is for managers only. The app never writes to the rota sheet. Managers are told as managers, never asked to cover.
